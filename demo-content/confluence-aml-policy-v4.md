# AML Policy v4 (approved)
SYNTHETIC DEMO: Northstar Pay (fictional). Approved by the Compliance Committee on 2 October 2026. Supersedes AML Policy v3. Owner: MLRO office.

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets how Northstar Pay detects, investigates and reports suspicious transactions on business accounts in Singapore. It applies to every analyst, investigator and system that handles a transaction-monitoring alert.

## 2. Red flags that require investigation
- **Rapid movement of funds:** several inbound transfers from counterparties not previously seen on the account, received within 48 hours, followed by onward transfers of most of the funds within days. Treat this as a high-risk red flag when the inbound total is more than three times the customer's expected monthly inbound value.
- **New overseas counterparties:** three or more new overseas senders in one alert window, especially from high-risk jurisdictions or with shell-company indicators.
- **Payment references that do not match the customer's business profile**, for example "consulting fee" or "investment" received by a wholesale trading company.
- **Structuring:** repeated cash deposits just below S$20,000 within a few business days.
- **Repeat pattern:** the same red flag on a customer with a prior related case.

## 3. Risk rating of an alert
- **High:** a rapid-movement red flag with onward transfers, or any red flag combined with a prior related case. High-risk alerts follow section 4.
- **Medium:** a single red flag with a plausible business explanation that still needs supporting documents.
- **Low:** activity explained by the customer's known profile or a documented seasonal pattern. Close the alert with a note on the case record.

## 4. Escalation and reporting
1. The investigating analyst escalates every high-risk alert to the **MLRO within 1 business day** of the alert, with a risk assessment that cites the alert data and the evidence used.
2. Where the MLRO agrees there are reasonable grounds for suspicion, Compliance files a **Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the Suspicious Transaction Reporting Office (STRO) via SONAR**. Draft the narrative with the STR Drafting Guide.
3. Every STR filing is recorded as a case in the Jira project **FINCRIME** after Compliance-lead approval. The case holds the approved STR narrative.
4. Place the customer's accounts under **enhanced monitoring** for at least 6 months after an STR is filed, with a monthly review of inbound and outbound transfers.

## 5. No tipping-off
Never tell the customer, or anyone outside Compliance and the MLRO office, that an alert is under investigation or that an STR is being considered or has been filed. Customer-facing teams continue normal service and refer questions to Compliance without explanation.

## 6. Record keeping
Keep alert records, investigation notes and STR copies for at least 5 years. Case notes and case files are restricted to Compliance.
