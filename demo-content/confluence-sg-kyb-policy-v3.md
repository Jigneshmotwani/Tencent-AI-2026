# SG KYB Policy v3 (approved)
SYNTHETIC DEMO: Northstar Pay (fictional). No real customer, regulatory or financial data. Approved 1 September 2026 by the Compliance Committee. Supersedes v2. Policy owner: Casey Compliance.

## 1. Scope
Applies to every Singapore business-account application reviewed by KYB Operations (Northstar Pay SG). Analysts record each requirement as Met, Missing or Discrepancy in the Jira KYB case.

## 2. Required documents
1. Completed application form signed by an authorised signatory.
2. Current company registry extract (ACRA business profile) dated within 30 days.
3. Identity document for **every** director listed on the registry extract. A director on the registry who is missing from the submitted director list is a discrepancy.
4. Shareholder register showing all shareholders and percentages.
5. Proof of operating address (utility bill, bank statement or tenancy agreement) **dated within the last 3 months**. Documents older than 3 months are not accepted. An address typed in the application form is not a substitute.
6. UBO declaration for every natural person holding 25% or more, directly or indirectly.

## 3. Ultimate beneficial owners (UBO)
- Threshold: any natural person owning or controlling **25% or more**.
- **Look-through rule:** for every corporate shareholder holding 25% or more, the analyst must obtain that entity's own shareholder register and identify its natural-person owners until all UBOs at or above the threshold are known.
- A corporate shareholder of 25% or more incorporated in a **high-risk jurisdiction** requires escalation to Compliance (enhanced due diligence) before approval.

## 4. High-risk jurisdictions and industries
- High-risk jurisdictions are those returned by the KYB registry risk rules service for SG (currently including Marovia and Eastland Federation).
- High-risk industries: money services, virtual assets, precious metals dealing, arms and defence.

## 5. Screening
All directors, shareholders and UBOs are screened for sanctions, PEP and adverse media.
- Score ≥ 0.85, or any sanctions potential match: **escalate** to Compliance immediately.
- Score 0.50–0.84: potential match; the analyst documents the review and includes it in the case memo. If other risk flags exist, escalate.
- Below 0.50: no action.

## 6. Requests for information (RFI)
- Raise one consolidated RFI per case listing every missing or discrepant item; use the approved RFI templates.
- The case moves to **Awaiting Customer** while the RFI is open. Chase after 3 business days; close as withdrawn after 15 business days without response.
- Never ask the customer for information about screening results.

## 7. Recommendation and escalation matrix
| Situation | Recommendation | Next status |
|---|---|---|
| All requirements met, no risk flags | approve | Ready for Approval |
| Missing/expired documents or discrepancies only | rfi | Awaiting Customer |
| High-risk jurisdiction corporate shareholder ≥25%, sanctions potential match, or score ≥ 0.85 | escalate | Escalated |
When both RFI items and escalation triggers exist, **escalate** and list the RFI items in the escalation.

## 8. Approvals
Only a human analyst or Compliance may transition a case or contact a customer. Automated assistants draft; humans approve.
