# Northstar Pay approved onboarding procedure
SYNTHETIC DEMO — no real financial or customer data. Approved 9 October 2026.

Acme Trading and other demonstration customers must provide the application, company registration, director identity evidence, and a company proof of operating address dated within three months. Missing address evidence blocks activation. An address typed in an application is insufficient.

Operations requests the missing document, records its receipt in the customer checklist and sends the completed checklist to Compliance. Only Compliance may mark compliance clearance. The app is a read-only assistant and cannot approve or activate processing.

Escalation: Olivia Operations at onboarding@example.invalid owns receipt and customer coordination. Casey Compliance at compliance@example.invalid owns policy interpretation and review clearance. Escalate a missing document unresolved for two business days to the onboarding desk; do not bypass the gate.
