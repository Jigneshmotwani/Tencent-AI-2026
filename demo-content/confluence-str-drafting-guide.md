# STR Drafting Guide
SYNTHETIC DEMO: Northstar Pay (fictional). How to draft a Suspicious Transaction Report narrative for filing with STRO via SONAR. Follow AML Policy v4 (approved).

## Template sections
1. **Subject:** one line naming the activity and the dates, for example "Rapid movement of funds, 12 to 14 Oct".
2. **Who:** the customer (name, customer id, account id) and the counterparties involved, by name and country.
3. **What:** the transactions: count, total amount, currency, and onward transfers.
4. **When:** the alert window with dates and times.
5. **Why it is suspicious:** the red-flag typologies that apply and the facts that support each one, with the evidence references used in the investigation.
6. **Prior history:** related alerts and cases, and whether an STR was filed before.
7. **Actions taken:** escalation to the MLRO, enhanced monitoring, and any account restrictions.

## Style
- State facts, not conclusions about guilt. Write "the pattern matches the rapid-movement typology", not "the customer is laundering money".
- Keep it to about 200 to 300 words.
- Do not include customer-facing language and never contact the customer (no tipping-off).
- Mask personal identifiers such as NRIC numbers and phone numbers in internal drafts; the final SONAR submission adds them from the case file.
