# RFI Templates
SYNTHETIC DEMO: Northstar Pay (fictional). Approved for use with SG KYB Policy v3.

## Standard RFI (customer-facing)
Subject: Northstar Pay business account: information needed for <Company name>

Dear <Contact name>,

Thank you for applying for a Northstar Pay business account. To complete our review of <Company name> (UEN <UEN>) we need the following:

<numbered list: one line per missing or discrepant item, stating what is needed and why, e.g. "Proof of operating address dated within the last 3 months (the document provided is dated <date>)">

Please upload the documents through the secure application portal. We will continue the review as soon as we receive them.

Kind regards,
Northstar Pay KYB Operations

## Rules
- One consolidated RFI per case. No internal risk or screening information in customer messages.
- Be specific: name the document, the person (for director IDs) and the reason.
- Do not promise approval or timelines.

## Director identity RFI line
"Identity document (passport or NRIC) for director <Director name>, who is listed on the company registry but not in the director list you provided."

## UBO look-through RFI line
"Shareholder register and UBO declaration for <Corporate shareholder>, which holds <percentage>% of <Company name>, identifying every natural person who owns 25% or more."
